# $JAXX2025 Tokenomic Model

## 1. Overview
$JAXX2025 is the primary utility, governance, and security token of the Valor Ai++//e ecosystem. It is designed to power the network, incentivize participation, and fund the core mission of veteran and whistleblower advocacy.

* **Token:** `$JAXX2025`
* **Total Supply:** 1,000,000,000 (1 Billion)
* **Max Supply:** 1,000,000,000 (Fixed Supply)
* **Model:** Deflationary. A non-recoverable burn mechanism is integrated into protocol operations to reduce supply over time.

## 2. Core Utility
1.  **Governance:** Stake `$JAXX2025` to vote on protocol upgrades, case validation, and treasury fund allocation.
2.  **Access & Payments:** Use as the native currency for all services, including AI-driven legal analysis and secure data vault attestations.
3.  **Security (Staking):** Staked by nodes and participants to secure the VALORCHAIN, ensure data integrity, and earn protocol rewards.
