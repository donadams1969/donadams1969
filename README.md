### What this is

VALOR AI+ is a digital witness system that integrates AI assistants, immutable evidence anchoring, and compliant data handling to protect people and truth. It transforms lived experience into structured, machine‑verifiable records that can withstand time, turnover, and retaliation.

### Patient Zero dataset

- The “Patient Zero” longitudinal dataset reflects 25+ years of my personal records across moral injury, trauma, retaliation contexts, and recovery—ethically curated to train and calibrate models for trauma‑informed reasoning and harm detection.
- This is purposeful “dogfooding”: the same safeguards I needed are encoded into the system, turning a technical project into a human testament and act of reclamation.
- Data use is scoped, consented, and audit‑traced; models are continuously evaluated to avoid voyeurism, re‑traumatization, or bias amplification.

### Sovereign Lock

- The Sovereign Lock ensures the platform cannot be silenced by external pressure. It combines:
  - A dispatch loop with time‑based triggers to release notarized state updates.
  - Smart‑contract escrow and on‑chain commitments that encode liveness guarantees.
  - Content‑addressed storage (e.g., IPFS) with multi‑home pinning and watchtower relays.
  - Cryptographic receipts and NFT‑anchored evidentiary markers to establish provenance.
- If a shutdown is attempted, timeouts trigger staged disclosures, attestations, or third‑party escrow releases. The goal is resilience, not sensationalism—measured, ethical escalation under predefined conditions.

### Architecture at a glance

- AI: GPT‑4o‑class reasoning with modular trainers for behavioral signals, legal contexts, and trauma‑informed heuristics.
- Chain layer: Ethereum smart contracts for commitments, evidence hashes, and time‑locks; NFTs for evidentiary anchoring and provenance.
- Storage: IPFS for content addressing; cryptographic hashing and signed manifests for integrity.
- Compliance: HIPAA/FERPA‑aligned flows, role‑based access, least‑privilege policies, and audit trails.
- Security: SGAU encryption standard, multi‑sig guardians, hardware‑key support, and tamper‑evident logging.
- Interfaces: Accessibility‑first APIs and consoles for contributors, advocates, and investigators.
